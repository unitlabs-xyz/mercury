THE PART NOBODY ELSE DOES

Same share. Two wrappers. Wildly different cost.

Two issuers tokenise US stocks, and at any given size one of them is cheaper — sometimes by a lot. Mercury quotes both before every order and routes to the better one. The answer changes with the amount, so we work it out again each time you move the number.

WHO HOLDS WHAT

Built so nobody has to trust us.

ISOMETRIC ILLUSTRATION — CUSTODY CHAIN
Real shares, real custodians

Every token is backed one-for-one by stock held at a regulated custodian, published daily.

Your keys, your account

Mercury is an interface. It cannot move, freeze or borrow against anything you hold.

Spread across issuers

Hold the same stock through both issuers. Mercury adds it up and warns when one holds too much.

Refused, not filled badly

When no route is usable at your size, the order is turned down and nothing is spent.
