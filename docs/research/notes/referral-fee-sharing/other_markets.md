# Referral fee sharing for a tokenized-US-stock app: rules in LatAm, Asia, the Middle East and Africa (as of 21 Oct 2026)

Scope note: Mercury sells Backed Finance xStocks (Swiss tracker certificates on US equities) from a self-custodial Solana app, charges a 0.5% fee, and plans to pay referrers 25-35% of that fee on friends' trades for 12 months, in USDC. This is not legal advice. Most sources below are regulator texts or law-firm and press summaries. Where only a secondary source was available, it is flagged. "Today" is 21 Oct 2026, so some deadlines in older sources have already passed and their outcomes are not verified.

A framing point used throughout (my inference, not a cited rule): in nearly every market below, a tokenized share or tracker certificate is analysed as a **security**, not as a "virtual asset" or crypto token. So the binding rules are usually securities-distribution rules (offer registration, broker/agent licensing, rules on who may solicit clients). The newer crypto/VASP regimes matter mostly by analogy, or because the app also moves USDC.

## Latin America: Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, Chile, Colombia. Are tokenized US stocks securities, and can a referral revenue share go to unregistered persons?

### Takeaway

Brazil is the clearest high-risk market. The CVM treats tokens that represent securities as securities (Parecer 40, Oct 2022). Res. CVM 178 reserves "prospecção e captação de clientes" (client prospecting and capture) for registered assessores de investimento, and the CVM has a record of stop orders against offshore platforms. A fee share paid to unregistered users there looks like irregular assessor activity. Argentina routes client capture and "referenciamiento" (referring clients) through CNV-registered agents, and lets PSAVs (virtual asset service providers) trade tokenized securities only inside a CNV regime. Mexico requires CNBV registration for public securities offers. Chile and Colombia were only lightly covered.

### Cited Findings

**Brazil: tokenized-stock status (CVM)**

- CVM Parecer de Orientação 40 was published 11 Oct 2022. A crypto-asset is a security if it "representa digitalmente" (digitally represents) one of the securities listed in art. 2, items I-VIII of Lei 6.385, or if it is an investment contract. Tokenization itself needs no prior CVM approval, but the issuers and the public offering of security tokens are subject to the applicable rules — [Mattos Filho](https://www.mattosfilho.com.br/unico/cvm-parecer-criptoativos/); [Capital Aberto summary via search](https://legislacaoemercados.capitalaberto.com.br/?p=19375)
- On foreign securities, PO 40 says an offer in Brazil "pode ser considerada irregular se não tiver registro na CVM" (may be irregular without CVM registration). For internet offers with no geographic restriction, the CVM points back to Pareceres 32/05 and 33/05: it looks at whether effective measures stop broad public access in Brazil — [Mattos Filho](https://www.mattosfilho.com.br/unico/cvm-parecer-criptoativos/)
- Enforcement against offshore platforms: the CVM issued a stop order telling Binance to suspend derivatives offers to Brazilians, on the basis that derivatives are securities and Binance had no authorization as a securities intermediary (daily fine R$1,000). The case was reopened after evidence that Binance told users to switch site region or language to keep trading. A similar stop order went to Bybit, which then stopped offering derivatives to Brazilians. Exact dates were not verified in these sources — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/linked/70481/brazil-binance-derivatives-regulators); [InfoMoney (Bybit)](https://www.infomoney.com.br/mercados/cvm-ordena-suspensao-de-servicos-da-exchange-cripto-bybit-no-brasil/)
- The CVM set up a working group to draft a tokenization rule and gave it 60 days to propose an experimental regime. The date and outcome were not verified — [Coinpaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/news/brazils-regulator-gives-tokenisation-task/)

**Brazil: who may be paid to bring in clients (Res. CVM 178/2023, in force 1 Jun 2023)**

- Art. 2, I defines the assessor de investimento as a natural or legal person "registrada na forma desta Resolução" (registered under the Resolution), acting "sob a responsabilidade e como preposto de intermediário" (under the responsibility of, and as agent for, an intermediary). Art. 3 says the activity covers "I – prospecção e captação de clientes" (client prospecting and capture), receiving and transmitting orders, and giving product information. Art. 11: the assessor "deve ser registrado" (must be registered). Art. 13: credentialing (with an accrediting entity) "é obrigatório". Art. 26, IV(b) puts the assessor firm's responsible director in charge of "prevenção do exercício de atividades previstas no art. 3º por pessoas não registradas" (preventing unregistered persons from carrying out the art. 3 activities). Art. 44 sets the effective date of 1 Jun 2023 — [CVM Res. 178 PDF](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/100/resol178.pdf)
- Res. CVM 179 adds remuneration transparency: intermediaries must disclose remuneration and conflicts online and send clients quarterly statements of commissions — [ANBIMA](https://www.anbima.com.br/pt_br/noticias/resolucoes-da-cvm-sobre-atividade-de-assessor-de-investimento-e-transparencia-na-remuneracao-passam-a-valer-hoje.htm)
- Enforcement on unregistered client capture: in the Nova Futura matter, the CVM treated as irregular an unregistered "assessor" who publicised a partnership with a broker and invited people to open accounts through a link pre-tagged with the assessor's ID. It also found the broker had not been diligent enough. The CVM fined defendants R$35.4m in a Sept 2024 case that included irregular assessor activity, and R$350k in a March 2024 case on irregular agente autônomo activity — [search summary of OKAI/CVM decisions](https://okai.com.br/noticia/2024-09-10/cvm-multa-em-mais-de-r-35-4-milhoes-acusados-por-operacao-fraudulenta-atuacao-irregular-de-assessores-de-investimento-e-oferta-publica-sem-registro); [OKAI Mar 2024](https://okai.com.br/noticia/2024-03-19/cvm-multa-em-r-350-mil-acusados-envolvidos-em-atividade-irregular-de-agente-autonomo-de-investimento)

**Brazil: finfluencers**

- The CVM ran public consultation SDM 04/2023 on finfluencers. Director Marina Copola said a stand-alone registered "finfluencer" category was unlikely and that transparency would be the focus. The CVM recommended that influencers paid by market institutions disclose those contracts — [Folha Vitória](https://www.folhavitoria.com.br/economia/para-diretora-da-cvm-consulta-publica-sobre-influencers-nao-deve-resultar-em-regulacao/); [B3 Bora Investir](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/noticias/mercado/cvm-recomenda-regulacao-de-influenciadores-digitais-veja-o-que-pode-mudar/)
- Self-regulation: in 2023 ANBIMA issued a manual for hiring digital influencers that requires a formal contract between member institutions and finfluencers — [Monitor Mercantil](https://monitormercantil.com.br/inclusao-de-influenciadores-digitais-no-radar-da-cvm/)

**Brazil: Banco Central VASP framework (Res. BCB 519, 520, 521, published 10 Nov 2025)**

- Res. 519 and 520 take effect 2 Feb 2026. Res. 521 (FX) mostly takes effect 2 Feb 2026, and its reporting and foreign-capital provisions on 4 May 2026. Existing PSAVs have 270 days to apply for authorization. Foreign providers must move their operations and clients to a local or BCB-authorized PSAV within 270 days or stop operating locally. From 30 Oct 2026, authorized institutions may not transact with Brazilian counterparties that are neither authorized nor in the authorization process (with exceptions). The three PSAV types are intermediárias, custodiantes and corretoras — [Mattos Filho](https://www.mattosfilho.com.br/unico/normas-regulamentacao-ativos-virtuais/)
- Under Res. 521, transfers to or from self-custody wallets fall within the FX market, and buying or selling fiat-referenced tokens is covered by FX rules. Mattos Filho's summary describes the stablecoin definition inconsistently, once as foreign-currency-referenced and once as fiat-referenced — [Mattos Filho](https://www.mattosfilho.com.br/unico/normas-regulamentacao-ativos-virtuais/)
- The Mattos Filho summary does not address marketing, incentives or third-party client acquisition in the BCB rules — [Mattos Filho](https://www.mattosfilho.com.br/unico/normas-regulamentacao-ativos-virtuais/)

**Argentina (CNV)**

- RG 994/2024 (Boletín Oficial, 25 Mar 2024) created the CNV PSAV registry under Law 27.739. It covers fiat-crypto exchange, crypto-crypto exchange, transfer, custody, and "financial services related to the offer or sale of a virtual asset". Registered PSAVs must show their registration number on their website and social media — [Blog del Contador](https://blogdelcontador.com.ar/news-43385-cnv-oficializa-regulacion-cripto-en-argentina-que-es-y-para-que-servira-el-registro-proveedores-de-servicios-de-activos-virtuales-psav); [Bruchou & Funes](https://bruchoufunes.com/nuevas-resoluciones-de-uif-y-cnv-sobre-proveedores-de-servicios-de-activos-virtuales-psav/)
- RG 1058/2025 replaced the PSAV regime. Per the search summary it was published 14 Mar 2025 and effective 26 May 2025, covering custody, segregated accounts and technology infrastructure. The dates are inconsistent across sources — [Boletín Oficial](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/pdf/aviso/primera/324012/20250624); [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/proyecto-para-tokenizar-valores-negociables-17160)
- Tokenized securities: the RG 1060 tokenization regime (in consultation in April 2025) was a one-year sandbox. Only PSAVs registered with the CNV would trade the tokens. The issuer needs CNV authorization, the underlying security must be immobilised at a CNV-regulated depository, and PSAVs may not use clients' tokens for their own account. A later "second stage" (RG 1081, per one source) admits tokenization of shares, including dual-listed shares, ONs (corporate bonds) and CEDEARs (local certificates on foreign shares). Whether foreign-issued certificates like xStocks fit was not addressed — [CriptoNoticias](https://criptonoticias.com/regulacion/exchanges-argentina-podran-ofrecer-acciones-tokenizadas); [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/index.php/la-cnv-amplia-el-regimen-de-tokenizacion-a-valores-negociables-con-cotizacion/37403)
- Client capture: under the CNV agente productor (AP) regime (RG 709), APs register with the CNV to act for one or more brokers (AN/ALyC). Their permitted functions include "captar clientes para su posterior alta" (capturing clients to be onboarded by the AN/ALyC), with the contracts available to the CNV. RG 1048 later regulated "referenciamiento de clientes, la publicidad de servicios (a través de terceros) y la canalización de órdenes" (client referral, advertising through third parties, and order routing), including referrals by ALyC/AN/AAGI to PSAVs — [CNV RG 709 (Consejo Salta copy)](https://www.consejosalta.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/CNV-709-1.pdf); [RankiaPro headline](https://rankiapro.com/latam/insights/comision-nacional-de-valores-de-argentina-reglamenta-referenciamiento-de-clientes-a-activos-virtuales/); [abogados.com.ar on consultation](https://abogados.com.ar/consulta-publica-acerca-de-la-reglamentacion-de-la-actuacion-de-los-agentes-de-control-y-revision-y-las-modalidades-de-captacion-de-ordenes-contacto-acciones-de-publicidad-y-difusion-de-servicios-de-los-agentes/35509)

**Mexico (CNBV / Banxico)**

- Mexico has no separate token category. Tokenized instruments that meet the definition of a security fall under the existing regime: registration in the Registro Nacional de Valores (RNV) and CNBV authorization for public offers. Private offers of unregistered securities are limited to statutory categories, such as institutional or qualified investors — [CryptoSlate law profile](https://cryptoslate.com/crypto-laws/mexico-securities-market-law-tokenized-securities-treatment/)
- Banxico Circular 4/2019 (DOF, 8 Mar 2019) governs virtual-asset operations by Fintech institutions and banks. Banxico cited price-formation and AML/CFT concerns — [DWF](https://dwfgroup.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2025/5/fintech-entities-and-virtual-assets-emerging-challenges-for-mexicos-financial-system)
- The CNBV granted case-specific no-action relief to Etherfuse (Resolution P090/2024) — [Etherfuse letter filed with US SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/etherfuse-brogan-law-052325.pdf)

**Chile**

- The Ley Fintec 21.521 (Jan 2023) brought investment advice, custody, order routing and similar services under the CMF. NCG 502 sets registration in the Registro de Prestadores de Servicios Financieros, with a section for foreign entities. NCG 503 covers proof of competence ("idoneidad") for investment advice — [Carey](https://www.carey.cl/api/archivo/nueva-ley-fintech-marco-regulatorio-open-banking-y-entrada-en-vigencia?lang=es); [CMF presentation](https://www.cmfchile.cl/portal/principal/613/articles-77286_recurso_3.pdf)

### Inferences

- **Brazil.** A user-to-user fee share for bringing in investors in tokenized US equities fits closely the art. 3, I activity of Res. 178 ("prospecção e captação de clientes"), which requires a registered, credentialed assessor acting as agent of an authorized intermediary. Mercury is not a CVM intermediary, and its referrers would not be registered. So in Brazil the scheme is better described as **effectively barred** than "restricted". The product offer itself is also exposed under PO 40, because a Portuguese-language, Brazil-targeted campaign defeats the Parecer 33/05 "effective measures" defence. Risk: **High**.
- **Brazil, practical note.** The BCB counterparty restriction (30 Oct 2026) means BCB-authorized PSAVs and banks may soon refuse to route funds to or from unauthorized offshore crypto services. That could hit fiat on- and off-ramps into Mercury even though xStocks themselves are securities under CVM jurisdiction.
- **Argentina.** Client capture for securities runs through CNV-registered APs, and referral and third-party publicity are regulated (RG 1048). Tokenized-securities trading is limited to CNV-registered PSAVs inside a CNV regime. A percentage-of-fees payout to unregistered users therefore looks **restricted (registered agents only)**. Risk: **Medium-High**.
- **Mexico.** Any "public offer" of non-RNV securities needs CNBV authorization. A mass referral programme aimed at Mexican residents would strengthen a finding of public offer. Risk: **Medium-High** (the specific rules on referrers were not verified).

### Gaps

- I did not find a CVM ruling that directly addresses a retail "indique e ganhe" (refer-and-earn) programme, or whether a one-off flat bonus to an existing client differs from an ongoing revenue share. Counsel should confirm whether occasional referral by a client is exempt from Res. 178.
- I did not verify the date of the CVM Bybit stop order, or any CVM action on tokenized-stock platforms (xStocks or similar).
- I did not confirm whether the CVM finalised any finfluencer rule after SDM 04/2023 (as of Oct 2026).
- Argentina: I did not verify remuneration rules for APs, the current RG 1048 text, or whether foreign tracker certificates fit the tokenization regime. Sources disagree on RG 1058/1060 dates.
- Mexico: I did not find the current Banxico Circular 4/2019 text or 2025 amendments, CNBV enforcement against foreign apps, or Mexican rules on paid "promotores" of securities.
- Colombia: no reliable sources found in this session (SFC crypto sandbox, foreign-offer rules).

## Asia: Singapore, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam, Turkey. Tokenized-stock status, introducer and referral rules, incentive bans

### Takeaway

Singapore and Hong Kong treat tokenized stocks as ordinary securities and capital-markets products, with prospectus and licensing rules for retail. Singapore's crypto guidelines (PS-G03, effective 19 Jun 2025) explicitly ban referral incentives to retail, which shows MAS's policy stance. India bars SEBI-regulated entities from any "money or money's worth" or client-referral association with unregistered advisers and return-claimers (Aug 2024 regulations, Jan 2025 circular). Turkey bans crypto platforms from giving benefits to customers who bring in customers (Sept 2024). The Philippines and Vietnam require local licensing and block or illegalise unlicensed platforms. Across Asia, an unlicensed percentage-of-fee referral programme ranges from restricted to banned.

### Cited Findings

**Singapore (MAS)**

- MAS issued the Guide on the Tokenisation of Capital Markets Products on 14 Nov 2025, replacing the Guide on Digital Token Offerings. Whether a token is a capital markets product (CMP) depends on economic substance. Offers of tokenised securities or CIS units fall under the same SFA Part 13 prospectus regime as non-tokenised ones, and complex-product safeguards apply where relevant. Prospectus exemptions include small offers (up to S$5m in 12 months), private placements (up to 50 persons) and institutional or accredited investors — [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/11/singapore-mas-publishes-guide-on-tokenisation); [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/12/guide-on-the-tokenisation-of-capital-market-produc)
- PS-G03 (revised 19 Sep 2024; consumer-access sections effective 19 Jun 2025), para 4.3.2: a DPT service provider "should not offer any incentive: a) to entice any retail customer to engage the DPT services … or b) to any person (e.g., existing customers or celebrities) to promote and/or refer the DPT service provider's DPT services to any prospective or existing retail customer". Para 4.3.3 lists sign-up, referral, trading and "learn and earn" incentives. Illustration 4 treats a benefit to an existing customer for a successful referral as a "referral incentive". Benefits the provider merely passes along, without initiating or controlling them, generally do not count (para 4.3.4) — [MAS PS-G03 PDF](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/guidelines/pso/ps-g03-guidelines-on-consumer-protection-measures-by-digital-payment-token-service-providers/guidelines-on-provision-of-consumer-protection-safeguards-by-digital-payment-token-service-providers.pdf); [Allen & Gledhill](https://www.allenandgledhill.com/sg/publication/articles/29214/mas-issues-revised-guidelines-on-provision-of-consumer-protection-safeguards-by-digital-payment-token-service-providers)
- MAS's consultation response confirmed the incentive ban covers sign-up, referral and trading incentives and extends to "learn and earn", despite industry arguments against a blanket ban — [MAS consultation response](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas/news-and-publications/consultation-papers/2022-proposed-regulatory-measures-for-dpt-services/response-to-public-consultation-on-proposed-regulatory-measures-for-dpt-services-part-2.pdf)
- Introducers: Regulation 31 of the Financial Advisers Regulations exempts persons carrying out "introducing activities" from FAA licensing. Notice FAA-N02 sets standards for FIs that appoint introducers: disclosure by the introducer, a script, no handling of client money, no advice or recommendations, and disclosure of whether the introducer is remunerated. MAS has proposed treating digital lead-generation firms as introducers — [MAS Notice FAA-N02](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/notices/notice-faa-n02); [MAS draft amendments](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas/news-and-publications/consultation-papers/annex-15-draft-amendments-to-the-notice-on-appointment-and-use-of-introducers-by-financial-advisers.pdf)
- On 22 May 2026, MAS responded to feedback on its advertising and introducer amendments. FI agreements with introducers must specify how consumer data is handled. Introducer ads fall under the Advertisement Regulations and need senior-management approval. Representatives who repost FI ads must disclose their identity. There is a nine-month transition from issuance of the updated rules — [Allen & Gledhill, 17 Jun 2026](https://www.allenandgledhill.com/sg/perspectives/articles/33082/sgkh-mas-responds-to-feedback-on-proposed-amendments-to-advertisement-regulations-and-introducer-notice-to-enhance-safeguards-for-digital-advertising-activities)

**Hong Kong (SFC)**

- The SFC circular of 2 Nov 2023 says tokenised securities are "fundamentally traditional securities with a tokenisation wrapper" (¶23). It drops a mandatory professional-investor-only restriction (¶26), but the prospectus and offers-of-investments regimes apply to offers to the Hong Kong public (¶27). Complexity is assessed on a "see-through" basis, and for complex tokenised securities suitability applies "irrespective of whether there has been any solicitation or recommendation" (¶24) — [SFC circular 23EC52](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/api/circular/list-content?refNo=23EC52&lang=EN)
- The revised VATP Guidelines prohibit gifts tied to trading a specific virtual asset, except fee discounts. The SFC described this as a principle applying to all intermediaries — [search summary of SFC consultation conclusions / Slaughter and May](https://slaughterandmay.com/media/z1wjejxt/new_regulatory_requirements_for_virtual_asset_trading_platform_operators_come_into_effect_4391pdf.pdf); [KPMG](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/cn/pdf/en/2023/06/sfc-consultation-conclusions-and-transitional-arrangements.pdf)
- Referrers: per law-firm commentary (secondary; I could not open the page), arranging introductions between a securities dealer and third parties for commission is treated as dealing in securities requiring a Type 1 licence, with an "introducing agent" category under the Financial Resources Rules — [Charltons, via search result](https://www.charltonslaw.com/?p=331). The SFC disciplined a licensed firm after an insurance-broker referrer that was paid commission on trades also performed regulated activities — [International Adviser](https://international-adviser.com/?p=1474)
- For comparison, Hong Kong's Insurance Authority (1 Sep 2025) set a supervisory benchmark that referral fees to unlicensed referrers should generally stay within 50% of broker commission. Fees tied to successful sales at 90%+ are a red flag, and using unlicensed referrers to conduct regulated activity is a criminal offence (up to HK$1m and two years) — [RPC](https://www.rpclegal.com/thinking/insurance-and-reinsurance/insurance-authority-takes-aim-at-referral-fees/); [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/article/3327432/hong-kong-regulator-puts-50-cap-referral-fees-insurance-sales)
- Enforcement: in Sept 2023, in the JPEX case (an unlicensed VATP promoted via KOLs, i.e. influencers, and OTC shops), police arrested influencers including Joseph Lam and Chan Wing-yee. The SFC told the KOLs to stop promoting. One later report says 16 people were charged, including seven influencers; I did not verify the date — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/197573/hong-kong-influencer-arrested-over-jpex-exchange-promotion); [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/56251/Six-arrested-in-crypto-probe); [MEXC news (secondary)](https://www.mexc.com/news/155234)

**India (SEBI, RBI, FIU)**

- Amendments to the SEBI Intermediaries Regulations, SECC Regulations and Depositories Regulations were published 29 Aug 2024 (one firm dates notification to 26 Aug 2024). They bar SEBI-regulated persons and their agents from associating, directly or indirectly, with persons who give unregistered advice or make unapproved return or performance claims. "Association" includes "(i) transaction involving money or money's worth; (ii) referral of a client; (iii) interaction of information technology systems" and similar arrangements — [MetaLegal](https://www.metalegal.in/post/sebi-strengthens-regulations-for-persons-associated-with-the-securities-market); [AZB & Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/sebi-restricts-certain-intermediaries-from-association-with-unregulated-advisors/)
- SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/11 (29 Jan 2025) clarified who is a "person regulated by the Board" and who is an agent. Investor-education-only persons are exempt if they give no advice or return claims. Per the FAQs, making or receiving payments, or sharing client information, counts as association. Branding and marketing with persons not engaged in the prohibited activities is allowed — [King Stubb & Kasiva](https://ksandk.com/newsletter/sebi-unregistered-investment-claim-makers/); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-bars-intermediaries-from-sharing-info-with-unauthorised-advisers-125012901368_1.html)
- Background: the SEBI board approved the framework on 27 Jun 2024, following the consultation paper of 25 Aug 2023. Outlook Business dates the paper to Aug 2024, which looks wrong. The proposals barred referral fees as trailing commission, for example on mutual funds bought through a finfluencer's link — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/investment/story/sebi-tweaks-regulations-for-finfluencers-removes-content-of-over-15000-unregulated-entities-443789-2024-08-31); [Freefincal](https://freefincal.com/how-new-sebi-rules-will-affect-the-income-of-finfluencers/)
- Under the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), resident individuals may remit up to US$250,000 per financial year for permitted current or capital account transactions, including investment abroad. Remittances for margin or margin calls to overseas exchanges or counterparties are excluded — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/share-market/lrs-limit-and-tcs-on-us-stock-investments); [Groww](https://groww.in/blog/liberalised-remittance-scheme); [RBI circular index](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=9756)
- Crypto: the FIU treats PMLA registration as activity-based, not dependent on presence in India. It issued show-cause notices to nine offshore exchanges in Dec 2023 and asked for them to be blocked. In 2024 it fined Binance about US$2.25m (₹188.2m). One Jan 2026 report says 25 offshore platforms have been banned since Oct 2025 — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/269551/indias-financial-intelligence-unit-issues-compliance-notices-to-offshore-crypto-exchanges-including-binance-kraken); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/business/markets/india-financial-watchdog-imposes-225-million-penalty-on-crypto-exchange-binance-1.1718881007051); [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/fiu-issues-notices-to-25-offshore-crypto-exchanges-over-aml-non-compliance/)

**Indonesia (OJK)**

- POJK 27/2024 took effect 10 Jan 2025, moving crypto supervision from Bappebti to the OJK. It covers exchanges, traders, clearing and custodians. It does not cover public offering or issuance of digital financial assets (AKD). An OJK draft of 19 Sep 2025 would cover public offers of AKD, including "backed" and "unbacked" tokenized assets — [SSEK](https://ssek.com/blog/indonesia-issues-draft-ojk-regulation-on-digital-financial-asset-offerings/); [HSF Kramer](https://www.hsfkramer.com/notes/fintech/2025-posts/ojk-assumes-regulatory-oversight-of-digital-financial-assets)

**Philippines (SEC)**

- SEC Memorandum Circulars 4 and 5 (Series of 2025, dated 30 May, effective 5 Jul 2025) cover any person that offers, promotes or facilitates access to crypto trading venues or intermediation. The marketing rules cover social media posts, events, ads, educational materials and airdrops. CASPs must be local stock corporations with at least PHP100m paid-up capital. Influencer obligations are uncertain: one account says influencers may promote only registered CASPs, while an SEC comment said the rules mainly target platforms — [BitPinas](https://bitpinas.com/regulation/sec-clarification-casp-rules/); [Sumsub](https://sumsub.com/blog/the-philippines-issue-new-crypto-rules); [FintechNews PH](https://fintechnews.ph/67951/crypto/sec-clarifies-new-crypto-casp-rules-are-not-a-ban-in-philippines/)
- Enforcement: in Aug 2025 the SEC flagged ten unregistered exchanges (OKX, Bybit, MEXC, KuCoin, Bitget, Phemex, CoinEx, BitMart, Poloniex, Kraken) and asked the NTC to block them. ISPs complied, and Bybit stopped new +63 registrations — [BitPinas](https://bitpinas.com/regulation/sec-flags-10/); [BitPinas (Bybit)](https://bitpinas.com/business/bybit-stops-ph/)

**Vietnam**

- Resolution 05/2025/NQ-CP (9 Sep 2025) sets up a five-year pilot. Only Ministry of Finance-licensed Vietnamese enterprises may run exchanges or market related products. Minimum charter capital is VND10 trillion, foreign ownership is capped at 49%, and all activity is in VND. Licensing opened 20 Jan 2026 (Decision 96/QD-BTC). One outlet reports that trading on unlicensed platforms becomes illegal six months after the first licence is issued — [LuatVietnam](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/resolution-on-piloting-the-crypto-trading-market-in-vietnam-for-five-years-892-103974-article.html); [Duane Morris](https://blogs.duanemorris.com/fintech/2025/09/12/vietnam-introduces-pilot-program-for-virtual-asset-market-what-you-must-know/); [The Asset](https://www.theasset.com/article/55719/vietnam-triggers-licensing-for-virtual-asset-exchanges)

**Turkey (CMB/SPK)**

- Law 7518 amended Capital Markets Law 6362 and put CASPs under the CMB. CMB Board decision i-SPK.35.B.1 (19 Sep 2024, no. 1484, Bulletin 2024/48) bans campaigns that "promise a certain return, direct investors to invest in certain crypto-assets, or provide a benefit to the customer or those who bring in customers through customer acquisition". Existing campaigns had to end within two weeks — [Mondaq bulletin](https://www.mondaq.com/turkey/fin-tech/1531394/bulletin-on-the-decision-of-the-capital-markets-board-regarding-crypto-asset-service-providers)
- Enforcement: the SPK blocked PancakeSwap and more than 60 sites under CML arts. 99/3 and 128/1(a). In March 2026 the Advertising Board sanctioned crypto firms over unsubstantiated "best price" claims and suspended one firm's ads — [iTiger](https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/2549313808); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/turkey/fintech/1792896/the-advertising-board-sanctioned-some-crypto-asset-companies-for-misleading-advertisements)

### Inferences

- **Singapore.** xStocks are most likely CMPs (debenture or structured-note type), not DPTs. PS-G03's referral ban therefore probably does not apply directly, but it shows clearly what MAS thinks of referral incentives in speculative retail products. On the CMP side, paying unlicensed users per trade to solicit Singapore retail would run into dealing-licence and prospectus questions. The introducer exemption applies only to introducers appointed by a licensed FI, who give no advice and disclose their pay. **Restricted (licensed-firm-appointed introducers only)**; risk **High**.
- **Hong Kong.** An ongoing percentage of trading fees paid to whoever drives the trades sits close to "inducing or attempting to induce" a securities deal. That points to Type 1 licensing, together with prospectus and offers-of-investments rules on any offer to the HK public. JPEX shows that influencers themselves face criminal exposure for promoting unlicensed platforms. **Restricted to licensed persons**; risk **High**.
- **India.** The SEBI association rules bind SEBI-regulated entities, not an offshore app. But they show that India treats paying referral money to unregistered promoters of securities as abusive, and any Indian partner broker or bank would be barred from such ties. Tokenized US stocks bought with USDC sit awkwardly with LRS/ODI channels and the VDA regime, and the FIU actively blocks unregistered offshore VASPs. Risk **High**.
- **Turkey.** The ban is explicit and covers rewards to customers who bring in customers, so Turkey is **banned** for crypto platforms. Tokenized securities would also fall under CML 6362 licensing. Risk **High**.
- **Philippines and Vietnam.** These are effectively closed to unlicensed offshore platforms (blocking, and planned illegality in Vietnam), so the referral question comes after the licensing question. Risk **High**.
- **Indonesia.** The framework is in flux and needs local licensing. Risk **Medium-High** (referral rules not verified).

## Middle East: UAE (VARA, SCA, DFSA, ADGM, Media Council, CBUAE), Saudi Arabia, Bahrain. Referral programmes, incentives, finfluencer and advertiser licensing

### Takeaway

The UAE is the only market reviewed where the regulator's published guidance describes almost exactly Mercury's design. VARA's 2024 marketing guidance treats a programme in which "the referrer gets a fixed percentage of trading fees" as a referral incentive that "would need to be approved by VARA as part of its licensing application". On top of that sit SCA finfluencer licensing (Resolution 10/RM/2025) and the Media Council's Advertiser Permit (mandatory from early 2026). The 2025 CBUAE law also brings tech and DeFi intermediaries into scope, with a transition that ran to Sept 2026. So the UAE is "allowed only for licensed firms with prior regulatory approval". Saudi Arabia has no retail crypto framework and states that virtual currency dealing is unlicensed.

### Cited Findings

**Dubai: VARA Marketing Regulations and Guidance**

- The VARA Marketing Regulations 2024 took effect 1 Oct 2024 and apply to all marketing "in or targeting the UAE", whether or not the marketer is licensed. Firms must ensure monetary or non-monetary incentives do not divert or mislead investors' focus, and incentives must run long enough not to imply urgency. Marketing must say investors can lose all their money and have no financial protection — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/uae-vara-issues-further-regulations-and-guidance-on-marketing-virtual-assets-and-related-activities-in-dubai)
- VARA Guidance on the Marketing Regulations (31 Aug 2024): the non-exhaustive list of incentives includes "incentives where the client refers another Entity to invest in a Virtual Asset or use an Entity's service". Any monetary incentive must show the ongoing charges that apply after the incentive period, with similar prominence — [VARA Marketing Guidance PDF](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/sites/default/files/en_net_file_store/VARA_EN_388_VER1.pdf)
- Case Study F.1 in the same guidance: "ABC VASP is launching a referral programme … the referrer gets a fixed percentage of trading fees generated by users that sign up using their referral code. … ABC VASP's referral programme would need to be approved by VARA as part of its licensing application and ABC VASP must comply with all conditions imposed by VARA" — [VARA Marketing Guidance PDF, p.49](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/sites/default/files/en_net_file_store/VARA_EN_388_VER1.pdf)
- Factors for "targeting the UAE" include GCC-wide campaigns (deemed to include the UAE), AED pricing, Emirati dialect, UAE imagery, UAE influencers, community channels aimed at UAE residents, and whether geoblocking is used. Marketing by entities not licensed by VARA must prominently state that they are not licensed or regulated by VARA. Paid influencers must label each sponsored post and confirm the promoted firm is VARA-licensed — [VARA Marketing Guidance PDF](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/sites/default/files/en_net_file_store/VARA_EN_388_VER1.pdf)

**UAE federal and onshore: SCA finfluencer licence**

- SCA Chairman Resolution No. 10/RM of 2025 (20 May 2025, effective the next day) requires natural persons who give investment analysis, recommendations or financial promotions through digital or traditional channels, on regulated financial products (including crypto assets), to hold an SCA licence. Reported eligibility: a certified financial analyst who is independent (not employed by a licensed FI). Fees are waived for three years. In Oct 2025 the SCA reported a 1,100% surge in applications. Free-zone firms are not directly subject, but DFSA and FSRA promotion rules overlap — [Hadef & Partners](https://hadefpartners.com/news-insights/insights/regulating-influence-the-uae-s-new-compliance-regime-for-finfluencers/); [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/uae-new-licensing-regime-finfluencers); [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/15l8xps-sca-launches-region's-first-finfluencer-licence); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/uae-sees-1100-surge-in-financial-influencer-licences-1.500300576)
- Trade press flagged that introducing brokers and affiliates of UAE brokers may be caught. The SCA definition reaches anyone promoting financial products, including crypto, via "various written or audio social media platforms" — [TradeInformer](https://www.tradeinformer.com/broker-news/sca-launches-finfluencer-license-ibs-and-affiliates-impacted)

**UAE Media Council: Advertiser Permit**

- Under the federal Media Law, an Advertiser Permit is required for paid and unpaid online promotion regardless of follower count. Residents get three years free; visitors must apply through accredited agencies. Promoting one's own products is exempt. The deadline was extended to 31 Jan 2026; Gulf News and Middle East Briefing describe 1 Feb 2026 as the date a valid permit is required. Reported fines are AED 5,000-10,000 for advertising without a permit. Cabinet Decision 42/2025 (violations and penalties) took effect 29 May 2025 — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2025/media/creators-uae-extends-advertiser-permit-deadline/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/new-uae-law-advertiser-permit-now-mandatory-for-influencers-and-creators-for-social-media-1.500427938); [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/uae-influencers-risk-aed-10000-fine-for-missing-advertiser-permit-deadline/); [Afridi & Angell](https://afridi-angell.com/uae-increases-regulation-of-influencers-and-finfluencers-new-rules-for-violations-and-penalties-of-media-content/)

**UAE CBUAE: Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025**

- In force 16 Sep 2025. Article 62 reportedly brings into the licensing perimeter anyone who carries on, offers, issues or facilitates a licensed financial activity, regardless of technology, including DeFi platforms, middleware and infrastructure that enable payments, exchange, custody or investment. A one-year transition ran to Sept 2026, with reported fines up to AED 1bn. Self-custody is reportedly not banned. These are secondary sources, not the Gazette text — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/uae-new-financial-law-defi-regulatory-scope); [Sumsub](https://sumsub.com/media/news/new-uae-law-applies-regulatory-oversight-to-defi-and-web3-projects/)

**DIFC (DFSA)**

- The DFSA financial promotions regime applies to Crypto Tokens as to other financial products. From 12 Jan 2026, firms do their own documented suitability assessment of tokens, replacing the DFSA "recognised list". Making or approving a financial promotion relating to a Crypto Token is within scope — [Simmons & Simmons](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/cmkpciqym00h8u7k8g0i8q6ao/changes-to-the-difc-crypto-token-regulation-from-12-january-2026); [Elliptic](https://www.elliptic.co/insights/dubai-international-financial-centre-introduces-crypto-token-regulation/)

**Saudi Arabia (CMA)**

- A standing committee chaired by the CMA stated that virtual currencies are illegal in the Kingdom and "no parties or individuals are licensed for such practices". It warned about social-media ads targeting Saudi residents (2018) — [Arabian Business](https://arabianbusiness.com/culture-society/402716-trading-in-bitcoin-illegal-in-saudi-arabia-officials-warn); [CMA press release](https://cma.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/PR/Pages/DC1.aspx)
- 2026 commentary says a comprehensive Saudi virtual-assets framework is still pending, with retail crypto restricted and tokenization work focused on real estate sandboxes. This is non-official — [An-Nahar](https://en.annahar.com/en/business-tech/265903/the-sovereign-token-the-2026-strategic-transition-in-the-arab-world)

### Inferences

- **UAE.** Mercury's design (a percentage of trading fees to referrers) is the exact fact pattern VARA addresses: **allowed only for a licensed firm, with explicit regulator approval of the programme**. However, xStocks are securities, so the competent regulator is more likely the SCA onshore, or the DFSA/FSRA in the free zones, than VARA. Either way, an unlicensed offshore app targeting UAE residents faces (i) the CBUAE Article 62 perimeter, now past its transition, (ii) the SCA finfluencer licence for any referrer who posts recommendations, and (iii) a Media Council Advertiser Permit for each promoting user. Referrers posting codes with performance claims would face personal exposure. Risk: **Medium** via a licensed UAE entity; **High** if unlicensed.
- **Saudi Arabia.** There is no licensed route for crypto-rail securities to retail, and the authorities publicly treat unlicensed crypto promotion as illegal. Risk **High**.

### Gaps

- I did not retrieve VARA's rule text showing whether tokenized securities are outside VARA's "virtual asset" definition (and so under SCA/DFSA/FSRA). Counsel should confirm.
- SCA rules on promotion and introduction of securities (introducer licensing) and SCA's tokenized-securities framework were not verified.
- I found no ADGM FSRA finfluencer or referral guidance in this session.
- I did not verify the CBUAE Article 62 Gazette text or post-September-2026 enforcement.

## Africa: Nigeria (SEC), South Africa (FSCA, FAIS). Tokenized stocks and paid referrers

### Takeaway

Nigeria's Investments and Securities Act 2025 classifies digital assets as securities and makes unregistered digital-asset exchanges illegal. The SEC has a 2021 record of ordering apps to stop selling foreign stocks to Nigerians unless they are registered. South Africa treats crypto assets as FAIS financial products. Courts there have characterised commission-style lead arrangements as FAIS "intermediary services", so paid referrers risk needing FSP authorisation or appointment as a representative.

### Cited Findings

**Nigeria**

- ISA 2025 explicitly recognises virtual and digital assets and investment contracts as securities, and puts VASPs, digital-asset operators and exchanges under SEC oversight. The SEC said in April 2025 that it is now an offence for an unregistered entity to run online FX trading platforms, and it treats unregistered digital-asset exchanges the same way — [Gazette NGR](https://gazettengr.com/isa-2025-bans-unregistered-digital-asset-exchanges-online-forex-platforms-sec/); [InvestData](https://investdata.com.ng/unlicensed-digital-asset-exchanges-online-fx-platforms-outlawed-under-isa-2025-sec/); [ICIR](https://www.icirnigeria.org/tinubu-signs-investments-securities-bill-into-law/)
- On 8 Apr 2021 the SEC said only registered exchanges may sell foreign securities to the Nigerian public. It directed capital-market operators to stop brokerage of foreign securities through partner apps, and named Bamboo, Chaka, Risevest and Trove — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2021/04/09/sec-online-trading-platforms/); [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/2021/04/09/nigerias-sec-orders-unregistered-investment-tech-companies-to-cease-offering-securities/)

**South Africa**

- In Oct 2022 the FSCA declared crypto assets a financial product under FAIS. Anyone who, as a regular feature of business, gives advice or renders intermediary services on crypto must be an authorised FSP (s.8) or an appointed representative (s.13); otherwise it is a s.7(1) contravention. Applications were due 1 Jun - 30 Nov 2023 — [Cliffe Dekker Hofmeyr](https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/en/news/publications/2022/Practice/Finance/finance-and-banking-alert-20-october-2022-fsca-declares-crypto-assets-as-a-financial-product-.html); [Fasken](https://fasken.com/en/knowledge/2022/10/crypto-assets-in-south-africa-are-now-legally-recognised-as-financial-products)
- A High Court decision held that "commission-style lead arrangements can be characterised as FAIS intermediary services", with consequences for enforceability. The FSCA has flagged referral models and trading signals as routes into unlicensed services. I saw only article teasers — [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/tag/referralarrangements/)

### Inferences

- **Nigeria.** xStocks offered via an app to Nigerians would likely be "foreign securities to the Nigerian public" and/or digital-asset securities under ISA 2025, needing SEC registration. Paid referrers widen the public offer. Risk **High**.
- **South Africa.** A per-trade percentage paid to referrers is the "commission-style lead" pattern the courts have treated as intermediary services. That would require referrers to be FSPs or representatives. Tokenized equities may also engage the Financial Markets Act. **Restricted (authorised representatives only)**; risk **Medium-High**.

### Gaps

- I could not read the full Moonstone and High Court analysis, General Code s.3A (financial-interest and referral-fee limits), or any FSCA guidance on referral fees to non-representatives.
- I did not verify the current SEC Nigeria rules for digital sub-brokers or foreign-securities apps, or any SEC Nigeria rule on tokenized stocks specifically.
- I did not check how the FSCA treats tokenized equities (FAIS vs Financial Markets Act).

## Cross-market comparison: tokenized-stock status, referral revenue share to unlicensed persons (allowed / restricted / banned), incentive bans, enforcement, practical risk

### Takeaway

No market reviewed clearly allows an offshore, unlicensed platform to pay unlicensed retail users an ongoing share of trading fees for referring investors in tokenized US equities. The best case is the UAE, where a licensed firm can run a fee-share referral programme with explicit regulator approval. Elsewhere it is restricted to registered agents or introducers (Brazil, Argentina, Singapore, Hong Kong, South Africa) or explicitly banned as a crypto incentive (Turkey, and Singapore for DPT services).

### Cited Findings

- UAE/VARA: a fee-percentage referral programme needs VARA approval within licensing — [VARA Marketing Guidance](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/sites/default/files/en_net_file_store/VARA_EN_388_VER1.pdf)

- Design implications for counsel to test (inferences):
  - A **trade-linked percentage** for 12 months is the riskiest form almost everywhere: it looks like transaction-based compensation for soliciting securities deals. Flat, capped, non-transaction-linked rewards, or rewards only to the new user (fee discounts), may fare better in some regimes. Hong Kong's VATP rules, for example, carve out fee discounts. But MAS PS-G03 and Turkey ban sign-up and referral benefits outright for crypto platforms.
